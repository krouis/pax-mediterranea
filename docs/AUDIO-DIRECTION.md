# Audio direction

Use original, compact motifs inspired by frame drums, plucked strings, reed-like timbres, restrained
horns, and low maritime pulses; do not claim archaeological reconstruction or repeat modern
cultural clichés. Carthage is warm and maritime, Rome steady and martial, future Greece bright and
agile, and Egypt ceremonial and layered.

Prefer 48 kHz Ogg/Opus plus compatible fallback, clean loop points, and music peaks near -14 LUFS
with effects below startling levels. Load music lazily after interaction. Browsers may block
autoplay, so sound starts only after a user gesture. The slice synthesizes short UI tones locally.
Future recordings must document author, source, license, modifications, and repository location.
Master, music, effects, mute, and reduced-audio preferences must persist.
