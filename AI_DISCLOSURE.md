# AI disclosure

Pax Mediterranea is an AI-assisted open-source project for study, experimentation, and learning.
Significant portions of its design, code, tests, documentation, art concepts, music concepts, and
content may be generated or assisted by AI tools.

All contributions remain subject to human review. AI output can contain technical, cultural,
historical, or licensing mistakes. Historical content must be checked against reputable sources;
generated assets must be reviewed for originality and redistribution rights. Maintainers remain
responsible for the final repository contents.

Contributors should disclose substantial AI assistance when relevant. No claim is made that
AI-assisted historical text is authoritative, nor that the entire project is AI-generated.
